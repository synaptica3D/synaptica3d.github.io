Synaptica

Synaptica is a free, interactive encyclopedia of neuroscience. Instead of just reading about a neuron, you can spin one around, click on its parts, and watch a signal move through it.

What's inside

History

From ancient Egypt to optogenetics: the people and experiments that built the field.

Action Potential

Fire an axon, watch ions cross the membrane, and see why the signal is all-or-none.

Types of Neurons

Five neuron shapes in 3D, with every part clickable and a signal you can fire.

How to use it

  • Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to pan.
  • Click any part of a model to read what it does in the side panel.
  • Try the experiments: change the stimulus strength, switch to a myelinated axon, or fire a neuron.

A note on accuracy

The 3D models are simplified teaching models, not microscope data. Sizes and speeds are exaggerated or slowed down so you can actually see what is happening. Use Synaptica alongside your textbook and lectures, not instead of them.
